# NOVA HRM Platform

A comprehensive, cloud-based **Human Resource Management (HRM) platform** designed to streamline and automate your HR processes, improve employee engagement, and ensure compliance for businesses of all sizes.

## Features
- **Employee Management**: Centralized employee records with personal details, job roles, performance metrics, and leave balances.
- **Attendance & Time Tracking**: Real-time tracking of employee working hours, shifts, and attendance.
- **Payroll Processing**: Automated payroll calculations, deductions, and tax compliance.
- **Recruitment & Onboarding**: Efficient applicant tracking, interview scheduling, and onboarding workflows.
- **Performance Management**: Goal setting, performance reviews, and feedback management.
- **Leave & Absence Management**: Simplified leave requests, approvals, and tracking.
- **Compliance Management**: Built-in tools for compliance with labor laws and regulations.
- **Employee Self-Service Portal**: Employees can access their data, submit leave requests, and download payslips.
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Actionable insights through customizable dashboards and reports.

## Benefits
- Save time with automated HR workflows.
- Scale effortlessly as your organization grows.
- Easy-to-use interface for HR teams and employees.
- Enhanced security to protect sensitive employee data.

## Use Cases
- Managing large and distributed teams across multiple locations.
- Simplifying HR operations for growing startups and enterprises.
- Supporting remote and hybrid work models.
